Vylo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

Vylo is a habit-tracking app in which your daily habits grow a village. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how to get rid of it. Vylo contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking.

1. What we collect

DataWhy
Email address and passwordTo create your account and sign you in. Passwords are hashed by our authentication provider and are never visible to us.
Full name and phone numberOptional profile details you enter at sign-up. Used only to personalise the app. You can edit them at any time in Profile.
Your habits, daily check-ins, streaks and Farm PlotsThis is the app's content. It is stored on your device and backed up to our server so it survives a reinstall or a new phone.
Purchase and subscription statusTo unlock what you have paid for and restore it on another device.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers, or any usage analytics.

2. Payment information

We never see or store your card details. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We receive only a confirmation of what you bought and whether the subscription is active, by way of RevenueCat, Inc., who manage subscription status on our behalf.

3. Who processes your data

  • Supabase — hosts the database and handles sign-in.
  • RevenueCat — records which purchases and subscriptions belong to your account.
  • Google Play / Apple — process payments and manage subscriptions.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone else.

4. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists.

You can delete your account from inside the app: Profile → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, habits, check-in history and Farm Plots from our servers and from your device. It cannot be undone.

Records of purchases may be retained by Google, Apple or RevenueCat under their own policies and for their own legal obligations.

5. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections. Database access is restricted so that each account can only ever read and write its own rows.

6. Children

Vylo is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or erase your personal data. The in-app deletion above satisfies erasure immediately; for anything else, contact us.

8. Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
