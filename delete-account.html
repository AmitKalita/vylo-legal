Delete Your Vylo Account

Last updated: 1 September 2026

From inside the app

  1. Open Vylo and tap your profile icon, top right of the map.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm by tapping Yes, delete everything.

This is immediate and cannot be undone.

If you no longer have the app installed

Email amitkalita941@gmail.com from the address associated with your account and ask for your account to be deleted. Your account and all associated data will be deleted within 30 days of your request.

What gets deleted

Your account, your email address, your name and phone number, every habit you created, your entire check-in history, and all of your Farm Plots. All of it is removed permanently and cannot be recovered.

What we cannot delete

Records of any purchases or subscriptions are held by Google Play, the Apple App Store and our payment provider under their own policies and legal obligations. Deleting your Vylo account does not cancel an active subscription — cancel that in your store account settings.
